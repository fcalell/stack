# Phone

## Goal
A stack phone app renders, builds and is judged like a web app: it styles and runs on a device,
stack holds a phone consumer of its own, and the design critic judges a phone screen on a render
it can drive and measure.

## Breakdown rationale
The five defects a spike hit come first, each one story, since without 01 and 02 no stack phone
app renders. The phone consumer (06) follows, so the harness (07) has a target in the repo.
Evidence for every story: `.helm/research/phone-harness.md`.
