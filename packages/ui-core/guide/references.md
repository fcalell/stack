# References

The measured executions behind each pattern's range live on that pattern's page
(`patterns/<pattern>.md`). Each page lists its Mobbin queries (web platform, deep search, one of
them in dark mode), the executions cited by their Mobbin link with what each shows and its
measured numbers, the `DESIGN.md` lines from `VoltAgent/awesome-design-md` where that corpus has
the product, and the span of the references beside the range the [rubric](./rubric.md) takes.

Measurements read from Mobbin's 768 px previews are approximate CSS px at 1440; the `DESIGN.md`
lines carry exact values from a product's own stylesheet and win when the two disagree. A unit's
references are the executions of the patterns it implements: open two or three of them beside
the render. Flow-derived executions (Lovable login, toast and onboarding; Notion and Coda
workspace flows; v0 login) cite the flow's link, since a flow exposes no per-screen link. A
pattern whose shortlist is thinner than the rest says so on its page, with what stands in.
