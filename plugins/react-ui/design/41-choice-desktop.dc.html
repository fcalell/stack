Picker

What Select is not: a pick that stands outside a form, in a row, a toolbar or a definition row, and applies at once. No FormField labels it, so its label names it aloud and titles its sheet. The trigger is the field box at its bar fit (FIELD fit bar, the toolbar control's height); its list a popover at its own width anchored to the trigger's end, an option two-line when it carries a description, a search first above six options. On touch it opens as a sheet.

selected · a member's role, applied on pick: the role is a pick, a Picker at the row fit centred in the row as an act (the value and a ChevronDown in a PILL_ACT pulled back at its end only, so the row's gap stands before it; no box), Ana's Owner plain trailing on the title line since it cannot change; Ema's is open (the press wash while open, as the more act's); the Picker's popover at its width, anchored to the pick's end, two-line options, Member ticked, Admin highlighted
