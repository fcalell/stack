Workspace

The workspace owns every project, database and deployment you create here. Its name appears in invitations, in the browser tab and at the top of every email the product sends on your behalf.

Renaming it is safe at any time. Changing its address is not: links already shared with clients, API keys scoped to the old address and webhooks pointed at it stop resolving until they are updated.

Workspace URL

Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, 3 to 32 characters. The old address redirects for 30 days.
